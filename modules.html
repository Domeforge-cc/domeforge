DomeForge Modules

DomeForge includes a comprehensive suite of geometry engines and visualization tools. Below is the full catalog of Classic, Patterned, and Exotic dome generators, along with the DomePlayer and SpherePlayer viewers.

Classic Geometry

Tetrahedron

“The Tetrahedron is the most basic of the Platonic solids… The default TetraDome truncated just below the equator works well… Taken to a maximum 16 frequency our TetraDome reveals how this platonic solid can evolve…”

Hexahedron / Cube

“This is the most familiar of the Platonic solids… A 4 frequency truncated at the equator produces a visually appealing yet simple dome… We have also introduced the X‑Wise primary panel subdivision option…”

Octahedron

“Many people visualize the Octahedron as 2 back‑to‑back square pyramids… DomeForge provides 3 ‘methods’ of Octahedron subdivision… A 7 frequency ‘equal primary’ OctaDome was used to design the end caps of the Belltown dome…”

Icosahedron

“The icosahedron is almost synonymous with ‘Geodesic Dome’… The ‘Equal Chords’ and ‘Mexican’ methods are popular… You can get a good approximation of the Biosphere using a 12 frequency, -.8 truncation…”

Dodecahedron

“The Duodecahedron is the most complex of the Platonic solids… A simple DomeForge duodecadome with a frequency of 2… At higher frequencies with the fullerene option activated some architecturally exciting domes…”

Patterned Geometry

Lamella

“Lamella domes have concentric rings… Lamella domes have regular and repetitive strut and panel sizes… Multiple varieties of Lamellas are selectable…”

Schwedler

“The Schwedler Dome is the extension of a simple ribbed dome… We have added a ‘Twist’ option… Multiple varieties of rib/bracing arrangements…”

Fibonacci Dome

“Fibonacci Dome explores spherical domes based on phyllotaxis point distributions… Hybrid triangle/quad panelization… Panel‑type IDs exported via JSON…”

ElliptiDome

“ElliptiDome explores domes and shells based on elliptical footprints… Frequency and truncation controls adapted to elliptical layouts… Panel‑type and strut‑type analysis in real‑world units…”

WarpSurf

“WarpSurf explores warped surface geometries… Edge curvature and twist controls… Full output bundle with solid, wireframe, colored panels…”

Exotic Geometry

Snub Cube

“The snub cube… one of the 13 Archimedean solids… Its surface consists of 24 snub triangles, 8 more triangles, and 6 squares…”

Snub Dodecahedron

“The snub dodecahedron is an Archimedean solid derived from the regular dodecahedron… As a dome or sphere, the snub dodecahedron produces highly regular yet visually complex layouts…”

Small Rhombicuboctahedron (SRCO)

“The small rhombicuboctahedron… 26 faces… Useful testbed for hybrid panel systems… Easy to classify and fabricate…”

Small Rhombicosidodecahedron (SRID)

“The SRID has 62 faces… Produces remarkably uniform triangular panels even at f=1… Supports both low‑frequency domes and higher‑frequency refinements…”

BiPyramid Dome

“Triangular and Pentagonal bipyramids… Johnson solids #12, #13, #14, #16… Reasonable dome geometries… Examples of composite polyhedron…”

Players

DomePlayer

“DomePlayer is a visualization and comparison tool tailored to lower‑frequency domes… Emphasis on buildable geometries… Provides a common visual language for discussing options…”

SpherePlayer

SpherePlayer is used to explore high‑frequency dome outputs generated by DomeForge modules. It provides interactive visualization of PNG renders, structural layouts, and module outputs.
