DomeForge

Create Advanced Dome Geometries in Minutes

Overview

DomeForge is a unified suite of geometry engines and visualization tools for anyone interested in exploring geodesic domes. It offers a powerful interactive dome builder GUI and produces detailed geometry output.

Inspired by Buckminster Fuller, Edward S. Popko's Divided Spheres, and an experiment to recreate a geodesic design tool using modern AI.

Free Downloads

Two DomeForge components are available at no cost:

  • IcosaDome – A fully functional geodesic dome generator based on the Icosahedron.
  • SpherePlayer – A visualization tool for exploring high-frequency dome and sphere geometries.
