Contact DomeForge

Questions, support, bug reports & suggestions

Send us a message

We welcome questions and feedback about DomeForge. Complete the form to prepare an email in your email application.

This opens your device's default email application with the message filled in. Review it and click Send there. Nothing is sent automatically.

Email us directly

If you prefer, send a message using your regular email service:

support@domeforge.cc

For technical issues, please include the module name, version, and steps to reproduce the problem. Please do not include passwords, license keys, or other sensitive information.

How it works

Our support address forwards messages securely to the DomeForge Support Team. We'll respond by email.
